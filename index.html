About

I'm finishing a PhD in Solar-Terrestrial Physics at Queen's University Belfast, where I build automated Python pipelines that pull decades of observations from NASA and ESA spacecraft, global GNSS networks and radio observatories, then clean, join and analyse them to measure how the Sun affects Earth's upper atmosphere.

Before that I took a first-class MSci in Applied Mathematics and Physics, spent a summer in audit at EY, and spent three months at the European Space Agency. I like problems where the data is noisy, the stakes are practical, and the answer has to be explained clearly to people outside the field.
